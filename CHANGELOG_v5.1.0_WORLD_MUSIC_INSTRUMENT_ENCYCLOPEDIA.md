# Version 5.1.0 – World Music Expansion & Instrument Encyclopedia

## World Music Atlas 1.0

- 18 regional and historical music worlds
- Medieval Europe and Renaissance as dedicated categories
- Viking/Nordic, Celtic, Japan, China, Korea, Middle East, India, Balkan, Slavic, African, North African, Latin, Andean, Western, Polynesian and Arctic profiles
- Genres, scales, modes, rhythms, vocal traditions, atmospheres, tempo ranges and production guidance
- Authentic, Cinematic, Modern Fusion and Experimental directions
- Optional cross-world blend
- STYLE output, Song Director, Music Theory Director and Project Manager integration

## Instrument Encyclopedia 1.0

- 77 historical and world instruments
- Search by name, sound, region, family and genre
- Frequency range, performance character and recommended uses
- Reliable or Experimental Suno status
- Excellent pairings and possible conflicts
- Instrument comparison matrix
- Frequency and role overlap warnings
- Palette output for STYLE
- Instrument Evolution and Relationship Graph integration

## Song Director AI integration

- Medieval and Mittelalter detection
- Hurdy-Gurdy, Lute, Theorbo, Rebec, Shawm, Crumhorn, Psaltery, Recorder, Frame Drum, Tabor and Medieval Harp detection
- World Music profiles can influence instruments, scales, rhythms, STYLE and producer reasoning
- New connected module states are captured by Project Manager

All features remain local and offline.
