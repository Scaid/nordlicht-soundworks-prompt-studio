# Version 1.10.0 – Instrument Library 2.0 & Randomizer 2.0

- Instrument database expanded substantially, with deeper country coverage including Germany and Japan.
- Added metadata fields: ID, rarity, weight and era.
- Added three randomizer modes: Intelligent, Balanced and Experimental.
- Experimental levels: Mild, Creative, Wild and Chaos.
- Wildcard controls for genre, instruments, voice, BPM and rare instruments.
- Main and sidebar randomizers stay synchronized.
