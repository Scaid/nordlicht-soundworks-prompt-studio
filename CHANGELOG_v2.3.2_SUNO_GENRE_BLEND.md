# Version 2.3.2 – Suno-Friendly Genre Blend Phrasing

The genre blend remains visible as a percentage in the interface, but exported STYLE prompts now use natural-language weighting.

Mappings:
- 90/10: `Primary with subtle Secondary elements`
- 80/20: `Primary with light Secondary influences`
- 70/30: `Primary with Secondary influences`
- 60/40: `Primary-driven fusion with strong Secondary influence`
- 50/50: `Equal fusion of Primary and Secondary`

No unsupported `Genre : Genre : 0.5` notation is generated.
